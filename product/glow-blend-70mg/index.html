What is Glow Blend (BPC-157 + TB500 + GHK-Cu)?

Glow combines the three most popular repair peptides: BPC-157 (10mg) for tendons, joints and the gut, TB500 (10mg) for muscle and tissue repair, and GHK-Cu (50mg) for collagen, skin and hair.

Together they cover healing from the inside and visible skin quality on the outside, in one vial instead of three.

Benefits

  • Whole-body recovery support
  • Firmer, more radiant skin
  • Joint and soft-tissue repair
  • Better value than buying all three separately

Possible side effects

  • Injection site redness or stinging
  • Headache
  • Mild nausea

Who it may suit

Adults who want recovery and skin results together.

Avoid, or speak to a doctor first, if you have

  • Wilson's disease or any copper metabolism disorder
  • Pregnancy, trying to conceive or breastfeeding
  • Anyone under 18
  • Active cancer or a history of cancer, unless your oncologist agrees
