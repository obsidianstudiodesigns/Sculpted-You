Questions, answered

How much is delivery?

A flat R100 courier fee on every order, anywhere in South Africa. We don't offer collections.

Why do I sign a declaration?

Before paying, you confirm you're 18 or older, that you've read the information and terms, and that you buy at your own risk. A copy of the declaration is kept with your order.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs through PayFast, South Africa's secure payment gateway. You can pay by card, Instant EFT and other methods PayFast offers.

Do I need to see a doctor?

We strongly recommend it. Start with bloodwork, and discuss any peptide with a qualified healthcare professional, especially if you have a medical condition or take medication.

How should I store peptides?

Keep unmixed vials in the fridge, away from light. Once mixed with bacteriostatic water, keep the vial refrigerated and follow the storage guidance in your info sheet.
