Questions, answered

Delivery, payment, storage and how ordering works.

How much is delivery?

A flat R100 courier fee on every order, anywhere in South Africa. We don't offer collections.

Why do I sign a declaration?

Before paying, you confirm you're 18 or older, that you've read the information and terms, and that you buy at your own risk. A copy of the declaration is kept with your order.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs through PayFast, South Africa's secure payment gateway. You can pay by card, Instant EFT and other methods PayFast offers.

Do I need to see a doctor?

We strongly recommend it. Start with bloodwork, and discuss any peptide with a qualified healthcare professional, especially if you have a medical condition or take medication.

How should I store peptides?

Keep unmixed vials in the fridge, away from light. Once mixed with bacteriostatic water, keep the vial refrigerated and follow the storage guidance in your info sheet.

What comes in my package?

Each peptide order ships with what you need to mix it: insulin syringes, alcohol swabs, a 3 mL mixing syringe with needle, the peptide vial and a vial of bacteriostatic water. See the reconstitution guide for the full list.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed after payment is confirmed and couriered to your door. Most main centres receive parcels within 2 to 4 working days. Outlying areas can take longer.

Is my order discreet?

Yes. Parcels are sealed and plainly packed.

Can I return a product?

Because of their nature, opened or used products can't be returned. If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours. See the returns policy for details.

Which GLP-1 should I start with?

Most people start with semaglutide, the gentlest and most studied. Tirzepatide and retatrutide are stronger. Compare them on our GLP-1 page and talk to your doctor.

Is my information safe?

We collect only what we need to process your order and declaration, and we handle it under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act. See our privacy policy.
