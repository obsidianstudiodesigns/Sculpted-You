Start with your bloodwork

We can't make anyone do it, but we always recommend it. A blood panel shows what you're short of, flags anything that needs a doctor first, and gives you a baseline to measure results against.

What to ask for

Ask your GP, or a pathology lab such as Lancet, PathCare or Ampath, for these tests. Many can be done from one blood draw.

Metabolic health

  • Fasting glucose and HbA1c
  • Fasting insulin
  • Lipid panel (cholesterol and triglycerides)

Organ function

  • Liver function tests
  • Kidney function (U&E and eGFR)
  • Full blood count

Hormones

  • Thyroid (TSH and free T4)
  • Testosterone or oestradiol
  • IGF-1, before growth hormone peptides

Nutrients

  • Vitamin D
  • Vitamin B12 and folate
  • Iron studies (ferritin)

Why it matters

Weight-loss peptides change blood sugar and digestion. Growth hormone peptides affect IGF-1 and glucose. Recovery peptides are gentler, but it still helps to know your starting point. If something comes back out of range, sort it out with your doctor before you start.

Repeat the panel after a few months to see what has changed.
