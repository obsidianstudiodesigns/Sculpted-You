1. Courier delivery

Every order is delivered by courier for a flat fee of R100, anywhere in South Africa. We don't offer collections.

2. When it ships

We pack your order once payment is confirmed. Most main centres receive parcels within 2 to 4 working days; outlying areas can take longer. You'll receive tracking details when available.

3. Packaging

Orders are sealed and plainly packed. Each peptide ships with insulin syringes, alcohol swabs, a 3 mL mixing syringe with needle and bacteriostatic water.

4. When it arrives

Put unmixed vials in the fridge as soon as your parcel arrives.

5. Damaged or incorrect orders

If anything arrives damaged, incomplete or incorrect, use the details on our contact page within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and photos. We'll replace the item or refund you.

6. Returns

For health and safety reasons, opened, used or unsealed products can't be returned or exchanged. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act are not affected.

7. Refunds

Approved refunds are paid back to your original payment method through PayFast, usually within 7 to 10 working days.
