1. Overview

Sculpted You respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. To contact our information officer, use the details on our contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, cellphone number and delivery address.
  • Declaration details: your SA ID or passport number, date of birth, signature, typed name, the terms version you accepted and the date and time of acceptance.
  • Payment details: handled by PayFast. We receive confirmation of payment but never your card or bank details.
  • Device storage: your cart and age confirmation are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device.

3. What we don't collect

The BMI calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your height, weight and result are never sent to us or saved.

4. Why we use it

We use your information to process and deliver your order, keep a record of your purchase declaration, communicate with you about your order, and meet our legal obligations. We don't sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

  • PayFast, to process your payment.
  • Our courier partner, who receives your name, cellphone number and delivery address to deliver your parcel.
  • Service providers who host this website or store order records for us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside South Africa

Some of our service providers, including our website host, store data outside South Africa. We only use providers in countries with adequate data protection laws or under agreements that protect your information as POPIA requires.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including restricting access to declaration records. We keep order and declaration records for as long as needed for the purposes above and as required by law (generally five years), then delete them securely.

8. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

9. Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. See our cookie policy.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
